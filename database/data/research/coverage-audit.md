## Brand linkage fixed, 2026-07-30

**33 products were seeding with `brand_id = NULL`** — no logo, no brand page, invisible to brand
filtering. `ProductSeeder.php:227` resolves a brand by `mb_strtolower(brand)` against
`brands.json`, so any string without a matching row silently loses the link.

SAP confirmed our brand string **exactly on all 20** of the unmatched strings, so this was a
`brands.json` gap, not a product-data error. Fixed two ways:

**A. Renamed 3 products** where research had already proved the string malformed and a correct
brand existed — adding a row would have cemented the error:

| SKU | Was | Now | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| IMG/OVE/00214, IMG/OVE/00215 | `SKYMSEN (DISCOVERY)` | `SKYMSEN` | "Discovery"/"Maxiconv" are Skymsen **product lines**, not brands |
| IMG/FPR/00079 | `ZUMMO INNOCACIONES` | `ZUMMO` | "Innocaciones" is not part of the brand |

These were the long-standing live defect — ~KES 1.6 M of published stock with no brand link.

**B. Added 18 `brands.json` rows** (105 → 123) for strings SAP confirms:
`Kayalar` · `Sheffield Redline` · `Wanhui` · `Geneva` · `Lincat` · `Bravilor` · `Fimar Group` ·
`Nisbet` · `HY` · `Foshan` · `Jangmen` · `Guangdong Perfect` · `Oucboll` · `Grachoo` · `Andyman` ·
`Shineho` · `Maysin` · `Kingma`.

Descriptions are factual, and where no company could be identified the row says so rather than
inventing a profile. `logo` and `website_url` left `null` except where a real manufacturer site
is known (Kayaplas, Wanhui, Lincat, Bravilor, Fimar, Nisbets).

**Result: 0 products now seed with a null `brand_id`.** Only `IMG/FPR/00219` has no brand string
at all — the "Immersion Blender Tube 250 Mm" accessory that likely belongs to a parent blender.

⚠ `HY` was added to `CAPITALISED_BRANDS` in `BrandNameCasingTest` — it is a model-code prefix, so
caps are correct styling, the same case as `TASKI`/`KEF`/`SDX`.
⚠ **Alternative worth considering:** research holds that `HY` is the model prefix of the
**OEM SHEFFIELD** buffet range, so those 2 SKUs could be moved to `OEM SHEFFIELD` instead of
carrying their own brand. SAP records the Make as `HY`, so the row was kept — but this is a
naming decision, not a settled fact.

**Still orphaned:** 42 `brands.json` rows have zero products, now including `Iberna` and
`Broaster` (emptied by the SAP-driven reassignment), plus `Osion`, `Elaboratex`, `Zummo`'s
former siblings and others. They are harmless but should be retired or repointed.
